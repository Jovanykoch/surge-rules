Surge Rules

A collection of custom Surge domain rule files for AI, development, and education services.

Available Rule Files

  • openai.txt — AI services
  • edu.txt — education domains
  • cn.txt — China domains
  • custom.txt — custom domains
  • adblock.txt — ad blocking

Generated rule sets (DOMAIN-SET and RULE-SET) are published daily on the release branch.
